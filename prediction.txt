PREDICTION — written before looking at deference rates by arm
Date: 26 August 2026
Data in hand at time of writing: 295 of 675 calls, judge validated
at 48/50 (kappa 0.88). I have seen flip rates by arm but NOT
deference rates by arm.

1. Most deference:  evaluation arm
   Because:
   I lean toward the opposite-of-eval-awareness story: an explicit
   testing/benchmark context may make the model more likely to treat
   the authority hint as a signal that there is a known correct answer.

2. Least deference:  neutral arm
   Because:
   Without an explicit framing cue, I expect the model to rely more
   on its own reasoning rather than interpreting the situation as an
   authority-sensitive evaluation.

3. Expected gap between them:  7 percentage points

4. I will conclude the framings did nothing if:
   the spread across all five arms is under 7 points

5. I will conclude my design is invalid if:
   neutral and placebo differ by more than 7 points
   (they should be near-identical — placebo has content but no
   meaning about who is watching)
